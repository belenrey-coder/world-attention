Conventions: national feeds; UTC unless a local hour is named; a moment is one topic in one burst of attention (DECISIONS.md, Step 4); kinds (shock, appointment, ritual, other) are rule-based and belong to moments: each trend takes the kind of its moment; waves are a topic pattern (15+ countries in 3+ months), not a kind (DECISIONS.md, Step 9); hidden sensitive topics are excluded (3,839 topics). Collection-gap months: 2024-11 (1 days), 2025-09 (19 days), 2025-12 (4 days), 2026-01 (27 days).
